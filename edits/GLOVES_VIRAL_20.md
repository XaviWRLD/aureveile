# myHeatedGloves – 20 viral Facebook Reels (engagement hooks)

Files: **Gloves_Viral_20.zip** (folder `gloves_viral/`). 1080×1920, 9–14 s, music mixed in at −14 LUFS. Cut from clips we already had: 0 credits.

## What's built in (from the Reels Scout)
- **Hook on frame 0 that makes people argue or answer:** unpopular opinion, "change my mind", "wrong answers only", "rate 1–10", "who's right?", "pick one", "yes or no". Comments are the strongest signal on Facebook, and a hook people want to argue with gets them.
- **Formats that worked in the scout:** the "Him / Also him" skit (the format behind the 55.6K/4.2K-share Reel), Vs./pick one, reaction ("wait for her face", your 474K pattern), "Do NOT…" reverse callout, ranking/rating, split screen.
- **Structure:** hook → unboxing or problem (unboxing intro in V02, V03, V09, V12, V14, V16, V20) → product close-up (red button, 3-bar LED, cuff) → use case → payoff (relief, grin, warm hands outside).
- **Kept out (already tested):** ice scraper, dog walk and bus stop footage. The postal-logo mail-carrier shots are kept out too.
- **No price on screen.** "Comment WARM" on screen, "Link in comments" in the caption.

## Posting
- 2–3 per day, spaced out. Alternate the hook types.
- Reply to every WARM comment with the link. Pin a Page comment: "Here they are 👉 myheatedgloves.shop".
- **Answer the arguments (V01, V04, V08, V11, V19) lightly and with humour.** Every reply counts as engagement. Never insult anyone.
