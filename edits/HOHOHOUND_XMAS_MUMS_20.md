# HoHoHound – 20 Facebook Reels for „Weihnachts-Mamas“

Files: **HoHoHound_Weihnachts_Mamas_20.zip** (folder `hohohound/xmas_mums/`). 1080×1920, 10–15 s, Christmas songs only, music mixed in at −14 LUFS.
Made from clips we already had. No new generation, 0 credits.

## Mass desire (the one thing every Reel sells)
A German Christmas mum wants **a Christmas the whole family remembers: everyone laughing, everyone together, the dog included, and the photos to prove it.** She is the one who makes Christmas magic every year. She wants to see that magic happen, and to be the one who brought it. The costume is just the shortcut to that moment.

Every hook calls out *Weihnachts-Mamas*. Every payoff shows the moment: kids freaking out, Oma laughing, Mama tearing up, the family photo.

## Facebook rules built into the edits
- The hook text is on screen from frame 0, over movement, never a still title card.
- 10–15 s so people watch to the end, with a payoff in the last third.
- One idea per Reel and a different edit style each time, so Facebook doesn't treat them as duplicates.
- Comment triggers: „MAMA“ for the link, plus votes (1–4, 🎅/🎄) and "Markiere…" for shares.
- R15 loops seamlessly, which helps rewatches.

## Posting
- **1–2 per day,** spread out (e.g. 12:00 and 20:00). Alternate the products and the edit styles.
- After posting, comment „Hier ist er 🎄👉 hohohound.shop“ as the Page and pin it. Reply to every MAMA comment with the link.
- After 5 days, look at which **edit styles** get the most comments and shares, and make more in those styles.
