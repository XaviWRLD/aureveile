## Why it works
1. **One template, posted many times, with freshly generated footage each time.** The same hook family, the same 4 beats and the same sound, but new scenes in every video. Repeating the format is fine. Reusing the same footage is what killed our 40.
2. **Hook = forbidden + identity:** "Do NOT show this to a DOG Mom…". A single line on screen for the whole video, white text with a black outline, ending in "…" and 3 emoji. There's no CTA on screen at all.
3. **Frame 0 is a mystery.** A shape under a Christmas blanket with a hand already on it, so the open loop starts instantly.
4. **The reveal lands on the music hit.** Reveal at 2.8 s / 4.0 s / 4.3 s, and the strongest audio onset sits within ~0.2 s of each one. Everything before the reveal is a slow build. Nothing is cut fast.
5. **Long, calm shots.** Only 3–4 cuts in 10–13 s, with each shot held 2–5 s. That's the opposite of our beat-cut montages.
6. **A visual gag inside the product.** The fake arms holding a gift make people go "wait, how?" and rewatch.
7. **The dog walks straight at the camera** at dog-eye level and ends in a face close-up. Cute and personal.
8. **Social proof shot:** a crowd of strangers kneeling and filming the dog (store aisle, Christmas market). It tells the viewer everyone loves this.
9. **Ends on two dogs in matching costumes.** It quietly sells buying 2 and gives a cosy closing frame that loops back to the start.
10. **Small fluffy breeds** (Maltipoo, Cavapoo, Chihuahua, Frenchie): the most-liked look for US dog moms.
11. **Retail "finds" framing** (Costco/Target store, boxed packaging, #targetfinds) makes it feel affordable and real.
    ⚠️ We must NOT copy this: suggesting a product is sold at Costco or Target is misleading, and it uses their trademarks.
12. **Caption:** one comment keyword ("DOG"), probably auto-DM'd, plus 6 broad hashtags. No story at all.

## What this means for us (vs. our 40 flops)
- Their videos: one hook, one reveal on the music hit, slow shots, crowd proof, a duo ending, fresh footage every time.
- Ours: recycled footage, beat-cut montages with many cuts, text that changed several times, engagement-bait questions, hourly posting.
