## After 48 hours
- Note which **hooks** beat about 2x your average views. Repost those on days 8–14 with a new first line of text, or try the same video with a different song.
- Drop the hooks that stayed under your median. Each niche hook (hockey moms, dog moms, ice fishermen) is a separate audience test.

## The "Comment WARM" CTA
Every video asks viewers to comment WARM, so be ready to reply fast with the link:
- On Instagram, use an auto-DM tool such as ManyChat.
- On TikTok and Facebook, reply to comments in the first hour. Quick replies and comment activity help the video get pushed further.

## Music note
The song is baked into these files. If a platform mutes one (common on business or shop accounts), re-upload it without music and add the same song, or a commercial-library alternative, in the app.
