# myHeatedGloves: Native Retargeting Ads (Meta)

All creatives are built from **real product footage** (no AI renders), styled to look like organic posts.
Files: `edits/native_ads/` (4 videos, 9:16) and `edits/native_ads/images/` (10 images, 4:5).
Music in the videos is original/royalty-free, so it's safe for paid ads (chart songs are not allowed in Meta ads).

Facts used everywhere (keep it this way): 3 heat levels · one button · 3× AA per glove (not rechargeable) · free shipping · **1 pair $54.95 · 2 pairs $89.95**.
No fake reviews, no "warms up in X seconds", no battery-life numbers, no deadline dates.

## Audience setup (retargeting)
| Ad set | Audience | Window | Best creatives |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 Warm | Watched ≥50% of any Reel + Page/IG engagers | 30 days | Video D, A · Img 01, 04, 08, 09 |
| 2 Hot | Website visitors (excl. purchasers) | 14 days | Video B, A · Img 02, 03, 10, 06 |
| 3 Hottest | Add to cart / Initiated checkout (excl. purchasers) | 7 days | Video B · Img 07, 02, 06 |

- Exclude purchasers (180 days) from all ad sets.
- Advantage+ placements, Feeds + Reels + Stories. Videos = 9:16, images = 4:5 (Meta crops for Stories).
- Start ~€5–10/day per ad set, 3–4 creatives each, kill anything with CTR < 0.8% after ~1,500 impressions.
- Objective: Sales → Purchase. CTA button: **Shop Now**.
