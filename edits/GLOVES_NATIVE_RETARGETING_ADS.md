# myHeatedGloves: Native Retargeting Ads, Desire-Led (Breakthrough Advertising)

Creatives: `edits/native_ads/` (4 videos, 9:16) and `edits/native_ads/images/` (10 images, 4:5). All built from real product footage, styled like organic posts, royalty-free music (safe for paid ads). **No price anywhere in the creatives or copy.** Price lives on the product page.

Facts we may state (keep it this way): one button · 3 heat levels · 3× AA per glove (not rechargeable, swap & go) · free shipping (description field only). No reviews/quotes we don't have, no warm-up times, no battery-life numbers, no deadlines. Raynaud's/always-cold angle always says "comfort product, not a medical treatment".

---

## The Schwartz strategy behind every ad

**1. Mass desire we channel (we don't create it):**
*"I want to stop suffering through winter and actually enjoy it again,"* with warm hands as the doorway to that.
Sub-desires by ICP: the always-cold person wants **relief** · the gift buyer wants **to be the one who gave the perfect gift** · the dog walker / commuter wants **to stop dreading the cold part of their day** · Dad wants **to stay tough and comfortable**.

**2. State of awareness: Product-aware → Most aware.**
These people have seen the gloves (Reels, site, cart). So we don't explain from zero. We **intensify the desire they already felt** and remove the last doubt ("do they actually work?").

**3. Market sophistication: Stage 3–4.**
Heated gloves exist, and "warm gloves" claims are everywhere. So the claim alone is weak. We lead with **desire + identification**, then prove it with the **mechanism**: *one click → pick 1 of 3 heat levels → no charging, just AA batteries.*

**4. Techniques used**
- **Intensification:** sensory pictures of the moment of relief ("that first warm moment on a freezing morning").
- **Identification:** the reader recognises themselves ("the person who's ALWAYS cold", "6am dog walk", "Dad").
- **Gradualization:** small believable steps (press → pick a level → warm), never one big claim.
- **Redefinition:** gloves → "the end of cold-hands winters".
- **Mechanism:** "one click / 3 levels / no charging" makes the promise believable.

1. Hot/Hottest: **Video B, Img v2-02 (split), Img v2-11 (touchscreen)** (remove the last doubt + strongest desire picture).
2. Warm: **Video D, Img v2-01 (bus stop), v2-03 (dog walk), v2-05 (gift)** (identification angles).
3. After 3–4 days, move budget to the 2 best by cost per purchase and rotate in the next untested angle (01, 03, 05, 06, 10, A, C).
4. Test headlines separately: one desire headline (1–8) vs. one identification headline (9–14) on the same creative.
