## 1. Bottom line

- **The winning skeleton right now:** an identity callout or caption-pair hook → a reveal (blanket, box, store) → a warm or public payoff, 8–15 s, with one line of text on screen and a one-word comment CTA.
- **What's new on 21 Nov:**
  - The **cover → reveal** format (blanket or sheet over a pet or product) is the strongest pet format.
  - The **"Babe stop buying stupid stuff, you're 40" / "Me:"** skit drove the biggest shares of both sessions (55.6K and 20.9K reactions, 1K+ shares each).
- **Burnt out in English:** store-floor laughing, a husband holding back his lunging wife, and "Babe the dog doesn't need that" (almost always under 800 reactions). Keep store-shock German-only, and lead with a reveal instead.
- **Product to act on:** 🚨 **the Christmas-tree dog costume for HoHoHound.** 5 Reels from 4 accounts in one session, and no German seller seen.

## 6. Caption & CTA patterns

- **One-word comment CTA is the norm:** "Comment TREE / PAW / WINGS / CAT / WARM". It brings the highest comment counts (Pawmas.US 319, Box Box 193, "Comment WARM" 481 on a heated cat pad). Your "Comment WARM" and „SANTA“ already fit.
- **Put the CTA on screen** over the payoff shot, not only in the caption.
- **Specific identity callouts in the first line** beat "dog lovers": "always-cold wife", "sideline dad", „Kleinhund-Mama“.
- **Leave one objection open** ("would a beagle keep it on?"). The arguments add hundreds of comments; answer honestly.
- **"Send this to / Tag someone who…"** goes with the highest share counts.
- **0–5 hashtags.** Many winners use none, and several put a bare link in the caption (with the keyword CTA plus a pinned link comment).
- **What flopped:** claiming it's "viral" or "everyone's talking about it" (a heated-gloves competitor got 21 likes), and product-on-a-table demos with no person or emotion.
- **Honesty:** don't name a real store as the seller (comments call it out), and keep product claims factual (no warm-up times, battery hours, temperatures or medical wording).
